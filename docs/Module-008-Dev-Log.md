# Dev Log Module 8

## Name: Bryan Fontanez

### Module: 8

### Date: 11/14/2026

#### Goals for this Module

- [x] Update the game's combat system to use 100 HP.
- [x] Develop the Rift Meter mechanic.
- [x] Add powerful abilities that players unlock through the Rift Meter.
- [x] Update the game presentation to explain the new mechanics.
- [ ] Test the updated mechanics with players and adjust the balance.

#### Progress

- **What I accomplished**:
  - I updated the player health system so everyone starts with 100 HP.
  - I increased enemy health and card damage to match the new health system.
  - I added the Rift Meter. Every time a player lands on a Rift Space, they gain 1 Rift Point.
  - After collecting 3 Rift Points, players unlock a powerful ability.
  - I came up with four Rift abilities based on damage, healing, movement, and affecting other players.
  - I updated my presentation to explain the new health system and Rift Meter.

- **Challenges faced**:
  - One challenge was balancing the new 100 HP system with enemy health and card damage.
  - I also had to think about how to make the Rift Meter rewarding without making players too powerful.

- **Solutions**:
  - I increased enemy health and card damage to fit the 100 HP system.
  - I made the Rift Meter personal to each player instead of using one shared meter.
  - Players need to land on Rift Spaces three times before unlocking an ability, which gives them a clear goal.
  - I created four different abilities so players can choose how they want to use their power-up.

#### Learnings

> I learned that adding new mechanics can make a game more interesting, but they also need to be balanced with the existing rules. Increasing health to 100 makes combat feel more like an RPG. The Rift Meter also gives players another goal besides reaching the end of the board.

#### Free Thinking

> I want the Rift Meter to become one of the main features that makes Deckbound: Realms different from other board games. Players have to decide whether to focus on reaching the end or landing on Rift Spaces to unlock a powerful ability. I also want to explore more ways for players to interact and compete against each other.

#### Next Steps

> I want to test the new 100 HP combat system and the Rift Meter abilities. I also need to update the physical cards and board to match the new rules. After playtesting, I can adjust enemy health, card damage, and the power-ups if anything feels too strong or too weak.
