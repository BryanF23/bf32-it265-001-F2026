**Selected concept and reason:**  
I selected Deckrush: Blitz because I like the combination of adventuring, collecting cards, and fighting enemies. I also like having four different sections because it gives the game a clear progression from the beginning to the end.

**Feedback that changed or confirmed my choice, if received (otherwise say unavailable):**  
Unavailable.

**Other ideas to keep for later:**  
Card Adventure and Four Sections could still be used for ideas about the game's progression and card collecting.

**First physical prototype boundary:**  
The first prototype will focus on the four-section adventure, basic card collecting, and fighting enemies. I will use simple paper cards and a basic board instead of focusing on artwork or complicated rules.

**One feature to defer:**  
Multiplayer will be deferred until the basic single-player gameplay is working.

**One risk to test next:**  
I need to test whether collecting cards and fighting enemies creates fun decisions and whether players understand what they are supposed to do.
