**Selected concept and reason:**  
I chose Deckrush: Blitz because I like the combination of adventuring, collecting cards, and fighting enemies. I also like having four sections because it gives the game a clear path from the beginning to the end.

**Feedback that changed or confirmed my choice, if received (otherwise say unavailable):**  
Unavailable.

**Other ideas to keep for later:**  
Card Adventure and Four Sections could still be used for ideas about the game's progression and card collecting.

**First physical prototype boundary:**  
The first prototype will focus on the four sections, collecting cards, and fighting enemies. I will use simple paper cards and a basic board.

**One feature to defer:**  
Multiplayer will be added later after the basic single player gameplay works.

**One risk to test next:**  
I need to test if collecting cards and fighting enemies is fun and if players understand what they are supposed to do.
